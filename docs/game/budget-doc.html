4 · Scope discipline (why the budget stays small)

The jam build was deliberately cut to three biases deep (present bias + anchoring primary, sunk cost supporting), two mascots as pixel SVG rather than commissioned layered portraits, and a single persistent screen instead of five scenes. The post-jam roadmap (Jan 7 Chroma) is where the optional $300–450 illustration push and the Souq Mode spend would land — each behind a named milestone, not assumed.

Regulatory note: the budget funds no user data, no third-party processing of personal data, and no paid distribution. The game's ledger is browser-local and clearable; all AI-generated material is disclosed to judges and on the itch page.
