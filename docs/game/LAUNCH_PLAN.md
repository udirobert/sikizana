# The Late Market — launch plan (Zoud submission, Sun 15 Nov 2026 23:59 AOE)

The thesis of this plan: **be audacious in claims, conservative in risk.** Every hour
goes into turning a finished game into a verifiable wedge, not into more game. With
three days left, bold *scope* is just a way to submit something broken at 23:50.

## The frame

> A ten-minute game, five chapters, two lawful markets, seventy tests, sixteen dollars.
> Its numbers are audited in public, and its ending hands you the real tool.

Every clause is checkable, which is what makes it safe to say. The submission title
becomes **"The Late Market — a playable demo of an AP integrity engine"**, and the
submission opens with the spec sheet rather than an apology for scope.

## Explicitly rejected

- **Arabic voice/subtitling.** The +5 rewards the instrument, not the translation, and
  the instrument now exists. A full Arabic pass is a string table the size of the game
  and a build-failure risk on Sunday. Say why in the submission — that sentence is
  itself evidence of judgement.
- **A fifth chapter or a third market.** Every chapter multiplies the balance rail and
  the test surface. The finale is the payoff; don't dilute it.
- **The $300–450 illustration push.** Replacing working art three days out, for a
  rubric with no polish column. That money buys nothing we are scored on.

## Order of operations

1. Moves 1 and 3 → suite green (`npm test -- --run`), `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run build`,
   `node scripts/build-itch.mjs` → commit → tag the build `jam-final`.
2. Move 2: draft the audit page from `RESEARCH_VALIDATION.md` + `GDD.md` §4a; link it
   from the game footer and the itch page.
3. Move 4: instrumentation + the post.
4. Move 5, after the build is signed off.
5. **Sunday before noon AOE: freeze.** PDF the docs, upload to itch, submit. No code
   after noon — Sunday afternoon belongs to the form, not to a refactor that eats the
   buffer.

## Rails that do not move

- Every in-game number stays inside the 25-point accuracy band, labelled fictional and
  range-indicative; the audit page is the proof, not a disclaimer.
- Souq Mode keeps its interest-free framing and its single zakat hedge. The audit page
  states the remedy terms and the zakat basis exactly as the game does.
- Nothing in the game becomes a payment promise, a guarantee, or a named provider. The
  door leads to a read-only product; it sells nothing in the fiction.
- Instrumentation is aggregate and anonymous, disclosed on the audit page.
