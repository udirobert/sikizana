## Title
Siki & Zana: The Late Market

## Tagline
A night market opens where your uncollected invoices sleep.

## Pull-quote

## Short description (1–2 lines)
A financial-literacy game. You keep a stall open for one week while a night
market buys every invoice you leave. Chase, or watch a stall light. Siki
explains. Zana collects. The street keeps the ledger.

## Long description
The Late Market is a narrative management game about the least dramatic
financial decision in the world: what to do about an overdue invoice.

You keep a stall open for three days. Leave an invoice, and a stall on the
street lights — the Factor, the Collector, or the Shark steps up and names
their price. Chase it, and a stall shutters. The week ends on one number:
what the street took.

Then the Claims Desk puts a number down first, and a three-year customer
goes quiet. The ending reads your week back in one line. Run it again and
move that line.

**Privacy.** The game stores nothing about you: your run lives in your browser only.
It counts how far a run got, which market you played, and whether a replay beat your
first — five anonymous fields, no cookies, no ids, no IP. Do Not Track switches it off,
as does `?notelemetry=1`. The arithmetic is audited in public, figure by figure.

**Two markets.** Play in pounds with the UK's statutory late-payment interest,
or in dirhams where interest is not on the table: the Gulf run prices lateness
with a contractual fee you agreed to in your own terms — and charges zakat on
what you never collected. Same week, same arithmetic, two lawful instruments.
The Souq path opens in Arabic — السوق — with the zakat line and remedy card
labelled in both languages.

Fictional offers, real arithmetic. No providers named, nothing sold — the
free ladder (Zana's reminders) always collects for free.

## Facts for the sidebar
- Browser HTML5, no install, single session (~10–15 minutes)
- Sound on recommended — every line is voiced; silent-first option included
- Accessibility: text-size options, simple-language mode, reduced-motion
  respected, colourblind-safe by construction (state is never colour-alone)
- Made with pre-baked AI voiceover (ElevenLabs) and original music bed
